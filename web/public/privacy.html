calibre-cloud

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

calibre-cloud reads your Calibre library from your own storage and shows it in your browser. Your library and books go straight from your storage to your browser. We never receive them, and we store nothing about you.

What the app reads from Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google and allow calibre-cloud to see and download your Drive files. The app asks for read-only access and nothing else: not your name, your email address, or your profile. With that access, the app reads:

  • the files named metadata.db anywhere in your Drive, including Shared with me, and the names of the folders above them, so it can suggest where your Calibre library is;
  • the names of the folders you open in its folder browser, so you can pick your library yourself;
  • the sizes and checksums of files in the library you pick, to tell when they change;
  • the contents of the library's metadata.db, its covers, and the book files you open or download.

It never creates, changes, or deletes anything in your Drive.

How it's used

Only to show your library in your browser, and to let you read and download your books. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, we don't use it to train AI models, and we don't share it with anyone.

calibre-cloud's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it goes

Your browser fetches everything above straight from Google. None of it passes through our server: not your book files, not metadata.db, not covers, not the names of your files and folders.

Besides serving the app itself, our server only helps you sign in:

  • When you sign in, Google sends our server a one-time code. The server swaps it with Google for a refresh token, encrypts the token, and saves it in a cookie in your browser. The server keeps no copy. It has no database.
  • When your browser needs to reach Drive, it sends that cookie back. The server decrypts the refresh token, gets a short-lived access token from Google, and hands it to your browser, which keeps it in memory only.

What stays on your device

To open your library quickly, calibre-cloud keeps some things in your browser's storage on this device: which library you connected, a copy of the library's metadata.db, covers, the last three books you opened, your place in each book, and your reader settings. None of it is sent to us.

Cookies

calibre-cloud sets cookies only to sign in to Google Drive: one that lasts ten minutes while you sign in, and the encrypted sign-in cookie above. Your browser sends them only to the sign-in part of our server, and page scripts can't read them. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and no analytics of any kind.

MEGA

When you connect a MEGA shared folder, its link stays in your browser. Your browser fetches and decrypts the library straight from MEGA. Our server is not involved.

Hosting

The app and its sign-in server run on Cloudflare, which handles requests to them as our hosting provider.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect in the app deletes everything it keeps on this device (reader settings stay), asks Google to revoke calibre-cloud's access to your Google account, and deletes the sign-in cookie.
  • You can also remove calibre-cloud's access at any time from your Google account.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything the app keeps on this device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
