calibre-cloud

Your Calibre library, in any browser

calibre-cloud lets you browse and read your own Calibre ebook library in a web browser, straight from the cloud storage it already lives in. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Open calibre-cloud

How it works

  • Connect the storage your library lives in. For Google Drive, sign in with Google and pick the folder that holds your library. For MEGA, paste a link to the shared folder.
  • Browse and search your books by title, author, series, and tag, with their covers.
  • Read EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3, CBZ, and other formats right in the browser, or download any format.
  • Pick up where you left off: the app remembers your place in each book on this device.

Read-only, and private

calibre-cloud only reads your library. It never changes, adds, or deletes anything in your storage. Your library and your books go straight from your storage to your browser, and are never sent to us or stored by us.

Read the privacy policy for exactly what the app reads and where it goes, and the terms of service for the terms of use.
